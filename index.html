Who We Are

Direct Vertical Control Over Every Needle, Stitch, and Seam

1

Yarn Lab Testing & Acclimatization

Every inbound yarn lot is acclimated in humidity-controlled bays and lab-tested for tensile strength, yarn count (Nm), color fastness, and elongation.

2

Computerized Flat Knitting

553 Shima Seiki and Stoll automated machines run digitally programmed CAD files with multi-gauge, intarsia, and cable capabilities.

3

High-Precision Dial Linking

Garment panels are linked stitch-by-stitch on automated circular dial linkers (3GG to 18GG), ensuring flat, elastic, durable seams that never fray.

4

Wet Processing & Eco-Softening

Low-water-ratio commercial washing drums with organic bio-enzymes remove spinning oils, stabilize dimensional shrink, and achieve buttery hand-feel.

5

In-House Physical & Chemical Lab

Tension testing, crocking, torque wash testing, and pilling box analysis ensure compliance with REACH, OEKO-TEX, and brand proprietary standards.

6

Steam Pressing & Dual Metal Detection

Vacuum steam tables shape each garment to precise spec sheets. Every item passes through dual-head conveyor metal detectors for 100% needle safety.
