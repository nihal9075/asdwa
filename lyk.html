Circular Economy

The LYK Closed-Loop Material Cycle

How post-industrial cutting scraps are regenerated into certified luxury knitwear.

01

Color-Sorted Collection

Pure cotton and wool cutting clippings are collected and sorted strictly by Pantone shades, eliminating the need for fresh chemical re-dyeing.

02

Mechanical Fiber Shredding

Clippings undergo mechanical garnetting to extract original long-staple fibers without harmful acid breakdown or bleaching.

03

Reinforced Core Spinning

Regenerated fibers are blended with GOTS organic cotton or GRS recycled polyester to produce strong, low-twist yarns certified under GRS.

04

Automated Flat Knitting

Knitted on ASDWA's computerized Shima Seiki machines into sweaters, cardigans, and beanies for commercial global retail.
