Zero-Carbon Initiative

450 kWp Rooftop Solar Installation

Our Narsingdi factory roof is crowned with a 450 kWp monocrystalline solar array that generates 100% of daytime operational electricity. By harnessing clean solar energy, ASDWA cuts reliance on national fossil fuels, prevents voltage fluctuations, and offsets over 380 metric tons of carbon annually.
