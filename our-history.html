CHRONOLOGICAL MILESTONES

The Evolution of ASDWA Fashion Ltd.

How two decades of disciplined investment and engineering rigor built Bangladesh's premier knitwear manufacturing plant.

Origin
2002

Inception of Manufacturing Operations in Bangladesh

Established dedicated knitwear production facilities in Bangladesh, centering on European export standards, gauge precision, and ethical artisan welfare from day one.

Modernization
2008

Adoption of Shima Seiki & Stoll Computerized Flat Knitting

Pioneered the transition from hand-flat machinery to Japanese Shima Seiki and German Stoll computerized flat knitting systems, unlocking multi-gauge capabilities from 3GG to 12GG.

Integration
2014

Eco-Wash & In-House Testing Laboratory Commissioning

Integrated low-liquor ratio washing and tumbling bays alongside our climate-controlled physical testing laboratory, enabling batch-by-batch shrinkage, pilling, and tensile verification.

Consolidation
2018

Consolidation into Unified 12-Acre Campus in Narsingdi

Unified all knitting lines, 380+ dial linking machines, finishing halls, medical dispensary, and daycare crèche into our flagship campus at Kandai Bus Stand, Madhabdi, Narsingdi.

Green Transition
2022

450 kWp Rooftop Solar & LYK Rapid Prototyping Cell

Commissioned a 450 kWp monocrystalline rooftop solar installation providing 100% daytime operational power. Launched the LYK Innovation Lab for 72-hour physical sampling and circular recycled yarns.

Present Day
2026 & Beyond

A Global Benchmark for Responsible Knitwear Manufacturing

Operating 553 flat knitting machines with 8.5M+ annual capacity. Trusted by Zara, Mango, Pull&Bear, Bershka, and Oysho with GOTS, GRS, RCS, RWS, and BSCI accreditations.
