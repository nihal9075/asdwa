450 kWp Rooftop Solar Powers Our 553 Knitting Machines

Harnessing the abundant sunlight of Bangladesh to manufacture knitwear with minimal carbon footprint.

Solar installations on the rooftop of our Madhabdi, Narsingdi plant supply clean daytime power to our 553 flat knitting machines, linking rows, and lighting grids. This drastically cuts reliance on the national fossil grid and guarantees steady power for uninterrupted precision.

Global Organic Textile Standard certifying 100% organic cotton fibers from farm to final garment, ensuring no chemical pesticides.

Global Recycled Standard verifying recycled polyester, acrylic, and recycled wool with strict environmental processing criteria.
